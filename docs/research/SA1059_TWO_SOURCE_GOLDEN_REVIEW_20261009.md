# SA10.59: Source-signed multi-image Golden and visual review preparation

2026-10-09. **Research evaluation and reproducibility PASS. Human visual identity Golden HOLD. Original Stage8 release condition HOLD. Production integration / Phase15 NOT RUN; production UNCHANGED.**

## Scope and immutable evidence

SA10.59 evaluates *two independent SHA-signed source portraits* (Raden and GC001), each against the original and three already-archived, byte-pinned SVG research candidates: SA10.55 (default faceless), SA10.56 (hair/outfit color allocation) and SA10.57 (protected internal contour optimization). There are **6 actual candidate Chromium scene replays**, plus two source-matte Chromium renders. This is a **two-image acceptance harness, not a representative 16/18-image full Golden corpus** and **not a human visual approval**. Unauthenticated images may be shown to human reviewers, but must not be treated as signed Golden regression data. Prior research artifacts and signed source images remain immutable.

The harness `tools/research/sa1059_golden_review_harness.py` verifies source SHA through the signed Stage8/SA10.58 authority, exact pinned SVG SHAs, actual expanded vertex counts, faceless SVG output rule, and Chromium 144 DPR1 PNG byte hashes from earlier stages. It derives original source part roles and stage8 silhouette as independent reference data. Tests compare original-source nonface RGB MAE, original-source owner-specific RGB MAE, actual rendered silhouette IoU, symmetric differences and one-pixel-tolerant boundary F1, while explicitly forbidding changed face or arm pixels *between consecutive research generations*. This last rule is only a regression guard: **unchanged source arm pixels across SVG generations does not guarantee arms already match the original image**.

## Human-review protocol

The private `sa1059_two_case_golden_review_board.png` compares the signed original, SA10.55, SA10.56 and SA10.57 for both characters at original 340×340 scene size. Separate enlarged `raden_detail_strip.png` and `gc001_detail_strip.png` expose identity-bearing hair, garment and silhouette problems. `sa1059_human_review_blank.json` and `sa1059_human_review_sheet.md` define per-character observations for: (1) faceless default without skin plate, (2) hair/head silhouette, (3) clothing and accessory features, (4) left/right arms and pose, (5) overall identity, (6) provenance / no generative or embedded-raster detail.

Every field is marked **PENDING**; no subjective score or human signoff is generated by automation. This tool does not permit reviewers or programs to override the Stage8 budget decision or deploy from the review form. Golden coverage also remains limited to **2 signed independent inputs**; additional diverse signed-source cases and an explicit human review are needed before a general quality claim.
